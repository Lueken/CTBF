01 Conviction

Why I Facilitate

Great reasons a couple would choose to engage with the process.

It’s hard. It’s stressful. It’s heart-wrenching. And yet, I believe in the process with all my heart. If I didn’t, I would stop recommending that couples go through it and I would stop facilitating them. I almost did stop a few months after my original training. Rites of Truth (the reading of the document) aren’t only difficult for the couple. It’s difficult for your Partner Guides as well.

02 Risk

Honestly

Are there risks? Yes.

There are always inherent risks in taking a stance of being vulnerable. Vulnerability, by definition in the dictionary, requires risk. We can’t be vulnerable if we don’t risk being hurt or disappointed. There is some risk. Every FTD is different and there is no guarantee of the outcome. But I can assure you with high probability the outcome if you don’t choose to pursue a Full Therapeutic Disclosure.
