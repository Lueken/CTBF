For the Partner

But why would she ever choose to sit and hear your Disclosure?

Those are some of the reasons you would write and present a Full Disclosure to your wife.

EIGHT REASONS
Why She Would Listen

The truth will also set your wife free.

1

She deserves to know the full and complete truth about the man she married. She has a right to know.

2

At some point in the process, she will be encouraged to forgive you for your betrayal because it is a healthy place for her to land. But she can’t forgive what she doesn’t know.

3

Like you, the truth will also set your wife free. She can’t make informed decisions about her future if she doesn’t have all the information. She’s still being held captive in the darkness of deceit.

4

Until you are fully honest with your wife, it’s as if she’s standing on the seashore with the waves rolling in and around her. Her feet sink in the sand. The sand shifts beneath her and there is no way to get a firm place to stand. It’s a constant balancing act just to stay upright.

5

Her story to this point has been based on lies and partial truths. After a Full Disclosure she will be able to rebuild a new life story based on what was actually happening in your relationship.

6

Hearing your Full Disclosure will help her validate her intuition and her sense of what’s real in your relationship. It can help offset the gaslighting she’s endured and can help give her back her voice.

7

Hearing your Full Disclosure can help calm her nervous system which is a starting point for healing.

8

It can also help her begin to grieve the losses of the relationship she thought she knew so she can start to rebuild herself and, hopefully, your relationship.
