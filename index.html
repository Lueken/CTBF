A Brief Warning

About me as a Disclosure Guide and the writer of all this information.

Before you wade into the rest of our website and see why we do Full Disclosure and how we do Full Disclosure, let me give you a brief warning about me as a Disclosure Guide and the writer of all this information. I am a Professional, Certified Full Disclosure Guide. I am a Professional Mentor. I am not a professional copy writer and I believe A.I. is the bane of our existence. I tend to be direct. I am honest (not to a fault because I don’t believe that exists). I can be hard with my clients when I need to be but I try never to be harsh. I have a low tolerance for addicts’ baloney and yet, since I was one for 45+ years, I have a certain empathy for their situation. I’m writing now to the betraying spouse since I’ve been there but if you’re the Partner, I hope you find help here as well.
